About

I am Devansh Garg, an M.Tech student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad (IIT Hyderabad), advised by Dr. Praveen Tammana. Before joining IIT Hyderabad I received my B.Tech in Computer Science from NIT Goa in 2025.

My research lies at the intersection of machine learning systems and computer networking, with a particular focus on LLM inference optimization, in-network computing, datacenter congestion control, and programmable data planes. Most of my work is evaluated on real testbeds, including BMv2 and Intel Tofino. A complete list of publications is available on my Publications page and Google Scholar.

I am looking for summer 2027 research internships in ML systems and networking.
