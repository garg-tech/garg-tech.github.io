Past

P4TVal: baseline and evaluation

IIT Hyderabad · Aug 2024 – Apr 2025

P4TVal detects unauthorized changes to the forwarding rules in a programmable switch. My part was the comparative evaluation:

  • Implemented REV (Zhang et al., IEEE/ACM ToN 2020), the prior state of the art, in P4 on BMv2 and Mininet, as the baseline P4TVal is measured against.
  • Compared attack-detection time as the network grows from 2 to 32 switches. P4TVal detects table-rule manipulation 10× faster, and its detection time stays constant while REV's grows with hop count.
  • Measured P4TVal's data-plane overhead on an Aurora 610 Intel Tofino switch: about 50 ns added per packet, over 5,000 packets.

PaperREV implementation

P4 · BMv2 · Mininet · Intel Tofino · PTF

P4Auth live demonstration

IIT Hyderabad · 2025

Built the live demo for P4Auth, a system that secures in-network traffic control by authenticating the messages it relies on, between controller and switch and between switches. The demo runs HULA load balancing in three configurations side by side: normal operation, a compromised switch, and the compromised switch with P4Auth in place. Acknowledged in the paper.
